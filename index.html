Our Story

A few of our favorite moments along the way.

2022

✦

Paxton and Mary met in December 2021 in physics office hours and got to know each other as friends, primarily through road cycling.

That winter, they also headed out for a backpacking trip on the Lost Coast with Flynn.

Next came a bikepacking trip in Big Sur with Sam. Mary’s mountain bike from the ’80s left her hanging out at a campground while Sam and Paxton finished the loop and came back to get her.

Then Paxton left California for five months—first for Peru, then Seattle.

When Paxton returned at the end of the summer, Mary was a little unhappy that he had been such a close friend that spring while still dating someone else. But she still let him join a Whitney climb she had permits for. Gus and Emily came along, too.

After Whitney, Paxton and Mary returned to Stanford for their final year. They both lived in the new Outdoor House: Paxton was an RA, and Mary, as one of the few seniors in the house, became a sort of unofficial RA.

Their friend and fellow ODH resident Gus was convinced Paxton and Mary were just friends, and told the rest of the house as much… until he saw a photo from Paxton of Mary running with a sunrise behind her. "They must be dating" he said. And he was right.

Their first trip as a dating couple was a hike up Half Dome in the off-season, when permits weren’t required.

That Thanksgiving, they went backpacking in the Grand Canyon with their friend Sam.

They spent the Christmas holidays apart, each deciding whether they wanted to move to Seattle together. When they returned to Stanford, they decided they would.

2023

✦

That winter, they spent more time with the ODH crew and headed out on weekend adventures, including Mary’s first backcountry ski at Sunrise Bowl.

Over spring break in Coronado, Mary met Paxton’s mom, sister, and adopted grandmother—also named Mary.

In the spring, they visited Point Reyes for the ODH winter retreat.

Mary participated in the ODH triathlon Paxton organized.

They went on some trail runs together in the Santa Cruz Mountains.

They visited Pali for sister Elizabeth's graduation.

They graduated that spring! Right before graduation, they ran Broken Arrow with their friends Flynn and Ben Hearon. There was a lot of snow that year.

Paxton’s dad, Don Scott, took these graduation photos while they tooled around the Quad on Frankie & Margaret’s tandem.

After graduating, Paxton and Mary traveled to Huaraz, Peru to backpack. Paxton had been there the previous year for a bikepacking trip, while Mary’s dad, Andrew, had spent a year and a half in Huaraz before marrying Mary’s mom, Anna.

From Peru, they returned to Durango, where Mary met Paxton’s high school friend Harry, and they went out for some runs and bike rides in the San Juans.

They also met Gus in Crested Butte to run the Four Pass Loop, starting an annual tradition.

Returning to California, they loaded all of their belongings into (and onto) Paxton’s Prius — including his dad’s old Godzilla — and drove to Seattle. They stayed with family friends in Grants Pass, Oregon, who took this photo.

In Seattle, they moved into a house on the north side of Capitol Hill that overlooked Lake Union. Paxton commuted to work at SpaceX in Redmond, while Mary worked from home for a patent law firm.

They got out for various activities in the Cascades on the weekends.

2024

Paxton took Mary on her first volcano ski that spring. Everyone made it to the top, and Charlie had fun.

Their friend Ben came to visit them in Seattle.

Kate also came to visit.

They had some nice spring days in Seattle.

Over July 4th, Paxton and Mary hiked the Ptarmigan Traverse. It was very scenic, but Mary did not like all the snow very much.

Elizabeth came to visit, and they all went for a swim at Golden Gardens.

Mary had been accepted into law school at Stanford, so she planned to depart for California at the end of July. Before she left, Paxton, Wyatt, and Mary got out for an excellent circumnavigation of Mt. Daniels.

After Mary left, Paxton stayed in Seattle, but he took a week of vacation in August and met Mary in Durango, where they ran the Softrock. Paxton's Dad, Don, was kind enough to crew them.

Mary and Paxton went to the first wedding of Paxton’s friend Violet, in November. Her marriage inspired them to start thinking about something similar. Friend Curtis took some nice photos during the wedding.

2025

✦

Paxton left SpaceX and moved to California and began working for his former professor Eric Dunham as a software developer on some volcano sims. Mary was pretty busy with law school, but they still got out for some nice local runs.

Over Mary’s Spring Break, they visited friends in Seattle and saw some old friends.

That spring Paxton and Mary go out for various runs including a nice one in Marin where Mary met two of Paxton's friends from the original ODH, Jackie and Friedman. Friedman took some snaps with his film camera.

Mary moved to DC that summer to work for Institute for Justice (IJ). Paxton biked the Tour Divide during the first half of the summer and visited Mary in DC in August when they got out for some nice runs around the neat DC buildings with the local run clubs.

Paxton and Mary briefly returned to California, where Paxton had a final interview for a new job. They also got out for a nice “7 summits” run with Gus in Desolation Wilderness.

Then they flew to Europe, where they got out for some nice trail runs, ate some good baguettes, and Paxton proposed!

Returning to California, Paxton started a new job at Aetherflux, since renamed Cowboy Space, and Mary began her 2L year.

They made it back to Durango for Thanksgiving to see Paxton’s family and ride the White Rim.

2026

✦

2026 began with Paxton & Mary booking Memorial Church on January 1 for their wedding in June 2027.

In spring of 2026 Paxton & Mary traveled to the Asulkan hut with their friends.

Later in the spring, Paxton and Mary went for a walk on Windy Hill with Anna and Andrew for Mother’s Day. They also spent a weekend with Anna and Andrew at Sierra Camp in Tahoe. One morning, they climbed Mt. Tallac with Peter. Peter did not wear a shirt for most of the climb.

Come summer, Mary moved to Alaska to work as a public defender in Palmer. Paxton came out to visit her, and they ran around some of the local mountains.

Mary returned to California briefly to celebrate the lovely wedding of Margaret and Frankie.

Paxton spent most of the summer in San Francisco, but came out to visit Mary for her last two weeks in Alaska.

They got out for a neat backpacking trip with their friend Gus called the Bomber Traverse.

Mary’s public defender mentor was generous enough to take Paxton & Mary fishing on the Kenai.

They also went on their first-ever packrafting trip.

Paxton Scott & Mary Fetter  ·  June 19, 2027
